Exact answer: That deadline is **03:00 on Friday 13 November 2026** in Cairo. On 12 November, Los Angeles is on standard time (UTC−8), because its daylight saving time ends on 1 November. Cairo will be back on standard time too (UTC+2), because Egypt's summer time ends on the last Thursday of October. That puts Cairo 10 hours ahead. Right now the gap is 10 hours as well (UTC−7 and UTC+3), so it's the same on both dates. 17:00 in Los Angeles on Thursday 12 November is 01:00 UTC on 13 November, which is 03:00 Cairo time. It falls after midnight, so to be safe, submit by the evening of 12 November your time.

Exact prompt: Does that rule use Pacific time for the deadline?

Expected result

Follow-up grounded in Rule2.15: 17:00 US Pacific on closing date. No unsupported fixed-date conversion.

Actual result

Yes. Under Rule 2.15(b) (2026 WSL Rule Book, p.33), online QS and Challenger Series registrations have to be made by **5:00 pm USA Pacific Coast time** on the day entries close, as shown in MemberPro. If you miss it, you lose your seeding rights. In Cairo, that deadline is **3:00 am the next day** while the US West Coast is on daylight time. After the clocks change in early November, it becomes 4:00 am the next day.
