## Adult journey

The original adult test drives registration, legal documents, membership payment through Stripe test UI, membership confirmation, event selection, insurance and event entry. With mainFlows enabled, it creates a separate QA event from the selected eligible event, in the same season and with its tour/location/format/fees, then opens that event for entries. This avoids closing the source event or confirming unrelated athletes.

The entry response's entryId identifies the exact roster row. The flow verifies Waitlisted before closing entries, closes entries through the manager UI, verifies that the same entry remains in the roster, and uses Confirm & charge. It verifies the persisted confirmation and Collected payment state. The intermediate Pending status after closure is not treated as a product defect.
