# Failure details and recording update - 14 October 2026

Only 11 affected scenarios were rerun: two persistence checks, five checklist groups and four main journeys. The full suite was not run. Earlier unaffected results remain timestamped in the combined report.

## Report format

Failed checks show Expected result, Actual result, recorded scenario/request steps, evidence and collapsed technical details. No illustrative steps or true/false outcome labels are used as the failure description. User-reported reproduction steps are distinguished from executed steps.

The consumed Single Event credit issue is now a FAILED check in the adult Single Event flow and appears in the Failed filter. Its source remains user-reported: some cards still offer Register and open registration instead of Upgrade membership after the credit is consumed. No second completed registration/payment is claimed.

## Recording changes

Removed the fixed seven-second/0.3-second trim. New clips retain the relevant action through the observed state, normal viewport pixels and aspect ratio. Browser layout is not zoomed or assigned a fixed viewport. Native maximized capture is used for the refreshed main recordings. Players have slowdown, optional captions, original-size links and a jump-to-result control where aligned evidence is available. Recordings show the browser content viewport, not toolbar/tabs or an impossible simultaneous view of a long page. Normal scrolling exposes lower fields.

Viewport screenshots avoid temporary full-page capture distortion during filming. A separate CDP full-page capture helper is compiled for future full-page evidence without changing the live viewport. Original source recordings are retained in artifacts.

Twenty-five browser failure checks have reviewed clips, including two explicitly labelled later read-only diagnostics of retained event cards. Two Waitlisted checks and two under-13 contact-lock checks still lack a clean focused clip; their assertions/screenshots remain and the report explains the limitation. The attempted retained minor-tab diagnostic could not start capture and is not published as evidence. API and email-inbox failures use response/search observations, not unrelated browser videos. Full recordings are only for the four main journeys.
