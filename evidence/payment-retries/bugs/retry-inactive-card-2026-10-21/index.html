In simple words

The card was declined. The card becomes unusable after that kind of decline. The manager pressed Retry payment → Proceed and saw no error, but no payment was tried. The history then says "2 card attempts · 2 declined" right above a line saying the payment was not attempted. The Retry option stays on the menu, so the manager can keep retrying with no effect. Only the athlete changing the card fixed the payment.

Related finding (separate ticket): no email at the first decline

In the 3 minutes after the decline the athlete received no email. Technical notes

  • A generic decline is classified needs_new_method; only retryable declines are retried automatically (wsl-competition-service/domain/payment_retries.py).
  • The pinned saved card becomes inactive after the decline, so payment refuses the manual retry with "not attempted", but the BFF still answers 200 with the seed list.
  • A card change from RETRYABLE or NEEDS_NEW_METHOD triggers an immediate re-charge (RETRY_IMMEDIATELY_AFTER_PAYMENT_METHOD_UPDATE, wsl-payment-service), which is why recovery needed no manager retry.

Acceptance criteria

  1. With an inactive pinned card, Retry payment is hidden or disabled with the reason "the athlete needs to update the card".
  2. A retry that ends "not attempted" is reported to the manager in the UI.
  3. The history summary counts only real card attempts; "not attempted" is listed but not counted as an attempt or a decline.
  4. The automatic re-charge after the athlete updates the card keeps working.
