WSL-9760: No immediate email after the first entry-fee decline

Jira: WSL-9760 · Medium · To Do · Created 21 October 2026.

Summary An athlete receives no immediate email when their first event-entry fee payment declines. The submitted-entry email is the only message around that time. The first actionable failure email is produced by the 24-hour reminder schedule.

This is a requested first-failure notification gap. Current dev version-16 rules explicitly configure notices at 24, 72, 120 and 168 hours, so the observed behavior follows the present configuration. It is not evidence that the reminder worker or SES failed, and no prior approved requirement for an immediate email is asserted. QA/user requested this behavior be raised as a bug on 21 October 2026; Product should confirm the immediate-notice contract and delivery SLA.

Expected / impact Persisting the first actual entry-fee payment failure should promptly enqueue an actionable transactional email, independently of the first scheduled reminder. It should identify the athlete, event, amount, failure/recovery guidance and deadline. A needs_new_method failure should request a new card; retryable failures should explain the next eligible retry where applicable.

Without that initial notice, athletes may believe their entry is proceeding normally and may not learn that action is required until the 24-hour reminder. This reduces recovery time. No customer financial loss is established.

Current policy and technical findings Local source inspection; deployed commit/image revision was not verified. Actual scoped dev scheduling and delivery results are recorded separately above. - Rules version 16: notifications [24,72,120,168]h; retries [24,72]h; grace 168h; short-notice cutoff 10 days. - competition/application/payment_retries.py:93, schedule_after_failure, pins first failure/rules/deadline and records retry eligibility; this path does not publish an initial athlete notice. - competition/domain/payment_retries.py:107, latest_notice_due, only selects configured elapsed offsets; there is no T0 notice in this policy. - Worker publishes scheduled notice at payment_retries.py:259 and grace-ended escalation at :269. - All seven later payment notifications were sent with SES IDs and received. This is not an SES authorization or deliverability defect. - Suggested direction: a distinct idempotent first-failure notice, separate from reminder counters; retain first-failure anchoring and existing reminder offsets. Extend the event/template contract if necessary rather than merely changing a placeholder or resetting the failure clock.

Short-notice nuance: when failure is inside the last 10 days, the effective deadline is already reached; retry-worker processing can flag/escalate without granting a fresh seven-day grace. That is not proof of a synchronous initial-decline email at the failure callback. This exception was source-reviewed, not reproduced live in these two fixtures.

Acceptance criteria 1. First persisted payment failure promptly enqueues one actionable account-holder email within an agreed delivery SLA, without waiting for the 24-hour reminder job. 2. Redelivered failure events or repeated scheduler passes do not duplicate the initial email. 3. Preserve pinned T0, deadline, retry rules and 24/72/120/168h reminder accounting. 4. Match guidance to needs_new_method versus retryable/action-required outcomes; do not invent an automatic retry for a non-retryable card. 5. No further reminders after successful recovery or another valid stop condition. 6. Define/coalesce initial failure and short-notice escalation to avoid contradictory or duplicate messages. 7. Verify actual test-inbox receipt, account-holder/guardian routing, event/amount/deadline/link correctness and notification audit IDs.
