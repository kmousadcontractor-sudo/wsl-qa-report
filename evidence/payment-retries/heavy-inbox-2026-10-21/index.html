New finding: retries can cross the short-notice cutoff Eight acceptance cases explicitly used version-16 rules: retry offsets 24/72h, notices 24/72/120/168h, grace 168h, cutoff 10 days.

An event starts at T0+12 days, so the short-notice cutoff is T0+48h while grace ends at T0+168h. Leave the 24h retry pending, then run the real worker with an injected clock: - At 48h: one execute command; not flagged. FAIL against strict stop-at-cutoff. - At 48h + 1ms: one execute command. FAIL. - At 49h: one execute command. FAIL. - At 71.999h: one execute command. FAIL. - At 72h, exactly cutoff+24h: no retry and entry flagged. PASS. - Normal control: execute at 24h, receive failure at 24.001h, then tick at 48h/72h. No 72h retry is scheduled past cutoff and entry is flagged at 48h. PASS. - Separate cutoff=24h and cutoff=72h variants dispatch a retry exactly on the cutoff. Two FAIL results.

Focused result: 6 failed, 2 passed. These eight cases are already included in retry-suite.xml, not additional tests.

Technical cause: competition/application/payment_retries.py dispatches when next_attempt_at <= now < deadline + 24h, before flagging. domain/payment_retries.py permits a due time equal to the deadline. This lateness tolerance conflicts with the requested strict short-notice stop. Deployed source revision was not verified. Tests use real application functions with fake collaborators and injected clocks; commands were captured, not published to Stripe. No actual post-cutoff charge is claimed.
