Key findings

Take over fails (500)

Confirming Take over on an inquiry held by another Tour Manager returns HTTP 500 "Something went wrong on our side" (reason aws.rejected). The inquiry stays with the first Tour Manager. "Assign to me" on an unassigned inquiry works. See AC-07.

Uploads stop on a busy inquiry

On SUP-1010 (21 files plus many unfinished uploads) every new upload returns HTTP 500 aws.rejected. At the same moment a newer inquiry (SUP-1016) accepts uploads, and the full attachment flow passes there (AC-15 to AC-17). There seems to be a per-inquiry limit that answers with a generic 500 instead of a clear message, and the reply is still sent without the files. See the rerun below.

Protections to confirm

Beach Marshal asks for a "not a robot" picture check before opening the inquiry (AC-12). Staff can only send a limited number of new inquiries to one athlete per day; the refusal is titled "You do not have access", which is misleading (AC-14).

How Athlete Care works today

Athlete

  • Support → Athlete Care lists their inquiries with status ("Waiting for your reply").
  • Create Inquiry: Topic (7), Athlete, Subject, Message (10+ characters). No file upload on this form.
  • Replies can carry up to 3 files (JPEG, PNG, HEIC, PDF, 10 MB each).

Tour Manager

  • Athlete Care inbox: filters by status, topic and assignee; sorted by latest activity.
  • A new inquiry is Unassigned; replying needs Assign to me. Another Tour Manager sees Take over.
  • New inquiry starts a conversation with an athlete.

Beach Marshal

  • Checks the athlete's account and earlier inquiries first.
  • Proposes an "Open a support ticket" card (topic, title, message, athlete) and waits for the athlete to confirm or cancel.
  • The inquiry arrives "via Beach Marshal" with a staff-only summary of what it checked.
