Tour Manager Settings and email checklist

24 Nov 2026 · Dev · Built from the code (wsl-frontend, wsl-messaging, athlete, competition, support and BFF services). Sign in as the Dev Tour Manager. For emails, use a fresh Mailosaur address on the mailosaurServerId server; Dev mail only arrives there.

Settings URL: https://app.dev.worldsurfleague.com/tour-manager/settings?section=<tab>

Part 1: Tour Manager Settings (6 tabs)

On every tab

  • The tab opens from its link (?section=…) and stays selected after reload
  • The breadcrumb reads "Settings › " and the title and description match the tab
  • Every save that asks for a Reason for this change blocks saving when it's empty ("Enter a reason for this change.")
  • Every change appears in that card's History (timestamp, user, action, reason)
  • Cancel throws the edit away; nothing is saved
  • A finance-only card is read-only for a Tour Manager without the finance role

1. Entry fees (entry-fees)

  • The grid shows fees by rating × region, with currency and tax per region, for the chosen Year
  • Edit, then Save: the new fee is shown, and a new event in that region/rating uses it
  • An amount that isn't a number is refused ("Some amounts could not be read")
  • A tax change asks for a reason (tax-changed hint)
  • Copy year: copy 2026 → 2027 shows "Replace 2027's entry fees with 2026's?", and Replace fees copies every cell
  • History lists "Entry fees · " changes

2. Memberships (memberships)

  • Prices by region for the chosen Calendar year, with currency and tax
  • Edit, then Save: the new price is shown on the membership purchase page and in the receipt email (see Part 2, rows 3–4)
  • Add from the previous year: "Add 2027 from 2026?"
  • Copy to calendar year, then Replace prices, then Mark as reviewed
  • An unpriced product shows Add
  • History "Membership prices · "

3. Insurance (insurance)

  • Products for the policy year: type, covers, events, price
  • + Add product: price must be a whole or decimal amount; events 1–max, or blank for annual cover
  • Edit, then Save, then Delete ("Delete ?")
  • Copy to calendar year
  • Documents drawer: Upload PDF, View PDF, Rename, Delete from library, add to plan, Move up/down, Remove from plan, Save
  • The visibility dropdown shows "During and after purchase" in full (WSL-10836)
  • The plan's documents show to the member at purchase and after, as chosen

4. Locations (locations)

  • The list shows the count ("LOCATIONS · N · NOT YEAR SPECIFIC")
  • Add new location: Name*, Country*, Time zone* required; Find the location fills the address, latitude and longitude
  • Format tokens (Name, Town, City, State, Country) change the Preview, and events show the location in that format
  • Edit location, Archive, Unarchive; an archived location can't be picked for a new event

5. Payment rules & engine (payment-rules)

This is the only Settings tab that changes emails

  • Payment rules: Edit Grace period, Notification schedule (Add/Remove steps, hours/days), Retries (Add/Remove), and Stop chasing before the event (days), then Save
  • Defaults show: retries at 24h, 72h and 168h; reminders at 24h, 72h, 120h and 168h; grace 168h; stop 9 days before the event
  • A new rule applies only to declines after the change; an entry keeps the rules in force at its first decline
  • Handling fee: Rate (0–max, up to 2 decimals), Applies to (Entry fees / Memberships / Insurance), Save; "In force" date shows
  • Apply to current prices: "Apply 5% to current prices?" with a Year, then Apply to ; prices update
  • Cancellation fee: Membership and Insurance amounts save, and a membership/insurance cancellation uses them (email rows 8 and 20)

6. Accounts & NetSuite (accounts)

  • Settlement accounts: each product has an account for "all other regions"; Add a region gives a region its own rule
  • A gap shows "These sales cannot be priced yet" (or "The next version … would leave these sales unpriced")
  • NetSuite mapping: Add a row; a region row may set one code and inherit the other
  • History for both cards

Part 2: Every email (24 live)

For each one, check:

  • it arrives
  • the subject and wording are right
  • names, event, amounts and dates are right
  • links open the right page and work once (expired links are refused)
  • it's in the athlete's language (try one in fr / ja / pt-BR / es-419)
  • it goes to the right person

Account and sign-in

  • 1. Email verification: sign up (or Verify email › Resend). The account's own address gets a link. Only on where verification is switched on (off in production).
  • 2. Password reset: Forgot password. Link works once and expires.
  • Google/Apple sign-in sends no welcome email (expected)

Membership and payment

  • 3. Membership receipt: pay for a membership. Amounts before tax, tax, total (WSL-10430).
  • 4. Membership active: adult self-registered, or parent-registered 13–17. Also on upgrade.
  • 5. Guardian approved: 13–17 self-registered, after the guardian signs. Goes to the teen.
  • 6. Guardian membership active: under-13 paid after consent was already done. Goes to the parent.
  • 7. Guardian approval completed: under-13 consent and documents done after payment. Goes to the parent.
  • 8. Membership cancelled: member, admin or hub cancels. States the refund owed.

Junior / guardian consent

  • 10. Under-13 consent requested: register an under-13. Guardian gets a consent link. Also check Resend.
  • 11. Under-13 pending consent: under-13 membership paid before consent. Guardian gets a link.
  • 12. 13–17 documents requested: teen registers and names a guardian. Guardian (no account) gets a link. Also check Resend.
  • 13. 13–17 pending documents: membership paid before the guardian signs.
  • 14. Guardian contact changed: staff change a guardian's email. The old address is told, with no link.

Documents and insurance

  • 18. Documents signed: a self-registered adult signs their last document.
  • 19. Insurance confirmed: buy per-event cover. Not sent for annual cover, or when the payer isn't the account holder.
  • 20. Insurance cancelled: staff cancel a policy. States the refund.

Event entry

  • 22. Entry received (waitlisted): enter an event. Goes to the account holder (the athlete if self-registered).
  • 23. Entry confirmed and charged: Tour Manager confirms the field and the charge succeeds. No email for a waived fee or a promotion.
  • 24. Payment action required (one email, 7 versions): use a declining Stripe test card, then check each:
    • declined, retry scheduled
    • retry declined
    • declined, no retry
    • last retry declined
    • grace period ended
    • authenticate (3-D Secure card)
    • update card
    • Timing follows Settings › Payment rules. Nothing is chased in the last N days before the event.
  • 25. Entry cancelled: account holder clicks Cancel entry. A minor's guardian gets a separate copy when their address differs.
  • 26. Refund decided: Tour Manager decides the refund. Skipped when "no refund" was already said at cancel.

Tour Manager messages

  • 27. Message to account holder: Tour Manager › entry › Message, channel Email (and through AL "message the account holder"). The Tour Manager's subject and body appear in the fixed frame, with the Tour Manager's name. In person sends nothing.
  • 28. Invite (Grant membership): Tour Manager's Grant membership. Link valid 14 days. Check Resend, and an existing account versus a new address.

Support

  • 29. Support ticket replied: staff reply, or staff open a ticket for someone. No ticket content in the email.
  • 30. Athlete replied: the person replies on a ticket. The Tour Manager holding it is told. Always in English.

Things that send no email (check nothing arrives)

  • Alternate moved up, a Tour Manager withdraws an entry, wild card
  • Entries open, final week, event started or called off, schedule change
  • Guardian consent and document reminders (defined but nothing sends them)
  • No athlete is copied (cc) on any email today

Open in the browser: two Dev windows.

  • Tour Manager window (signed in): the 6 Settings tabs, plus Events, Athletes, Athlete Care and Finance.
  • Member window (signed out): Register, Login, Forgot password, Resend verification, Support and Legal.
