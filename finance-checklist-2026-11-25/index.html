Finance tab checklist

Dev, 25 Nov 2026. Tour Manager › Finance. Use November 2026: today's test purchases and refunds are there (October is empty).

What you'll find in November: about 30 real Stripe test purchases and refunds from today. They cover memberships in USD and AUD, insurance, entry fees, refunds after a cancellation fee, and partial refunds. In the athlete Payments tabs, open Jordan Hale…, QA Cancelfee… and Jordan Override….

How to mark each row: ✅ pass · ❌ fail (add a screenshot) · ⏭ not run (say why)

1. Page and filters

  • Revenue and Insurance tabs both open
  • Month picker: October shows "No transactions"; November 2026 lists today's sales and refunds
  • "Months run midnight to midnight in Pacific Time" is true: a sale made at 23:30 PT on the last day of a month falls in that month
  • Filters Tour, Regions, Event and Products each narrow the list, and the totals change to match
  • Combining filters (for example Memberships + AUS/OCE) shows only matching rows
  • Clearing filters brings everything back
  • "Sales and refunds as of …" shows the current time and updates on reload

2. Amounts are right (compare with the athlete's Payments tab and the receipt email)

  • Membership, North America: gross 262.50 USD = 250 base + 12.50 handling (5%), tax 0
  • Membership, Australia: in AUD, with GST split out (for example AUD 367.29 = 333.90 + GST 33.39)
  • Insurance: amount matches the plan price plus handling
  • Entry fee: matches the entry fee plus handling
  • Refund rows are negative and match the refund in the athlete's Payments tab (for example −212.50)
  • Cancellation fee kept: sale 262.50, refund −212.50, so the net kept is 50
  • Handling kept on a refund shows as "Handling Retained" for memberships (for example 12.50). Note what insurance shows.
  • Partial refund: one sale row and one refund row, not two refunds
  • Totals at the top equal the sum of the rows shown

3. Settlement account (which Stripe account the money went to)

  • US membership → WSL North America
  • Australian membership → WSL Australia AUD
  • Insurance → WSL Insurance
  • Europe and South America memberships → WSL Europe / WSL South America
  • Note: on Dev, all USD regions share one Stripe test account; on production each has its own. Check the name is right, not the Stripe key.

4. Downloads

  • Download CSV: opens in Excel; one row per sale or refund line; columns are clear (date, athlete, product, region, account, gross, tax, handling, net, Stripe ID)
  • Each CSV row has a Stripe ID (pi_… for payments, re_… for refunds)
  • CSV totals = on-screen totals for the same month and filters
  • Download as of the same time gives the same numbers as the screen at that moment
  • Download payout tie-out: one row per Stripe payout, with gross, Stripe fees and expected payout
  • NetSuite columns say unmapped (no codes are set in Settings › Accounts & NetSuite)
  • Names with accents (Tomás, Inês) look right in the CSV

5. Insurance tab

  • Lists insurance policies sold in the month, with plan name, athlete and amount
  • Cancelled or refunded policies show the refund and the broker fee kept (with an insurance cancellation fee set)
  • Note any extra USD 20 or USD 0 "purchase" rows after an insurance refund (seen in today's test)
  • Download works and matches the screen

6. Athlete Payments tab (opened for today's QA athletes)

  • Each payment shows date, product, amount, status (Paid / Partially refunded / Refunded) and the invoice
  • After a cancel with refund: status Partially refunded and the refunded amount (check it isn't listed twice)
  • A granted (free) membership shows USD 0.00 Paid, with no Cancel button (known: it can't be cancelled)
  • The invoice downloads and its amounts match Finance

7. Access

  • Tour Manager 101 (Finance role) sees Finance
  • A Tour Manager without the Finance role can't see Finance, or sees it read-only (note which)

Notes

  • Write down any amount mismatch with the athlete name, product, the on-screen value and the CSV value.
  • Don't change Settings while testing: the handling fee and cancellation fee are back to their normal values (5%; membership USD/AUD 50).
