In short

  • Every Tour Manager and athlete page loads, and both assistants (AL and Beach Marshal) answer.
  • Dev changed today in ways that broke our test scripts, not the product: documents are now accepted with a new call inside the membership step (the old call returns 410), entries with a third-party policy need an acknowledgement tick, the emergency contact fields became required ("*"), cards are saved per regional Stripe account, the Dev Tour Manager account also has the Finance role, and only 2027 memberships are on sale (test events moved to 2027). All scripts were updated and the affected tests rerun.
  • After the script updates and reruns, all 30 tests pass, including the browser tests for adult, 13–17 and under-13 registration, event entry and the junior check. Dev was slow at times today (the app took up to about a minute to load in a fresh browser), so load waits in the tests were raised.
  • Guardian consent email for 13–17 sign-ups: checked separately on fresh accounts; it arrives within 2–10 seconds. Earlier runs that saw no email had not reached that step.
  • Assistant follow-up (3 runs each): AL handles "star it, then archive it" correctly (the earlier empty answer was a one-off). Beach Marshal offers an Athlete Care inquiry when asked to delete the account; that looks like correct behaviour and the test expectation is out of date.
  • Seen on Dev and staging, small: the New event page calls /tour-manager/events//overview (empty id) and gets 404.
