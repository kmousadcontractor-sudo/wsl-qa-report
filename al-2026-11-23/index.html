What AL can do (from the code)
  • Who gets AL: a token with role admin or tour_manager. Everyone else gets Beach Marshal. A finance-only account does not get AL.
  • AL never changes data itself. It proposes a plan of signed steps (HMAC bound to the user, valid 1 hour). The manager confirms each step and the BFF runs it as that manager through /api/assistant/actions (Idempotency-Key required; a step runs once).
  • Read tools (pilot policy): seasons, event search and lookup by name, event detail/overview, entries (withdrawal reasons and contact details removed), entry and event history, tours, regions, locations, rule book search and rule by number, refund assessments, settings reads (fee grid, handling rate, payment rules, memberships, insurance, GL mapping, settlement accounts), field charge preview, refund and wild-card quotes, navigation to Tour Manager pages. No web search or places under pilot.
  • Write actions AL can propose (all need confirmation): create, copy, update, star, archive events; change event status (rules enforced by the service; standby, live and completed are system-only); cancel a scheduled opening; seed order; move alternates up and undo; withdraw (reason required), promote, waive a fee (reason or label required), retry a card payment, message an account holder (sends a real email); confirm and charge the field (charges cards); wild-card slots and assignments; seasons and season copies; tours; locations; money settings (fee grid, memberships, insurance, payment rules, handling rate, GL mapping, settlement accounts), which also need the finance role.
  • Hidden from AL: refund decisions and the refund queue, entry create/update, insurance documents, staff and admin actions. AL never decides refunds.
  • Limits: 2,000 characters per message, 30 messages per conversation, 10 runs a minute, 200 a day, one answer at a time per conversation, 45-second run deadline, 20 model calls and 30 tool calls per run, bulk plans up to 50 steps, sequences up to 10.
  • Guardrails: fixed refusals for crisis, wellbeing, medical, substances, coaching, travel logistics, other people's private lives and "ignore your instructions"; answers in the person's language; says it is an AI; tool output is treated as untrusted; client-sent system messages are dropped.
