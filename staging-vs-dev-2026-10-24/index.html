Real problems on staging

High · bug report written

Declined card payments and retries never settle; they stay "pending"

A successful first charge works (Collected, Confirmed). Everything after it never comes back: a card that declines on Confirm & charge stays pending forever, with no "declined" line in History, and Retry payment is never offered. A retry after the athlete changes card also stays pending. In one case a payment was collected but the entry stayed pending.

  • AUTO-PAY-002: Payment shows Declined. Dev PASS, staging "pending"; History has no decline line.
  • AUTO-PAY-010: the declining athlete is Declined. Dev PASS, staging "pending".
  • AUTO-RET-005: Collected with the new card. Dev PASS, staging "pending" with 0 captures.
  • AUTO-RET-006 / 008: failed retries recorded and Retry still offered. Dev PASS; staging records nothing and offers only Send message and Withdraw entry.

Evidence: event La Marginal Classic 01a16e79-2dc0-754a-b10f-15a27c35b3cf, charged 15:13 UTC; entry 01a16e79-4bce-… still pending two hours later. Likely cause: staging doesn't process Stripe's failed and retried payment results (webhook or worker). Report: artifacts/bugs/staging-declines-and-retries-stay-pending-2026-10-24.md

Medium

Tentative is refused for an event with no rating and no entry fee, and the error names no field

Staging accepts Tentative when the event has a rating or a fee, but refuses it when both are blank: "Some of the event's details do not fit together. Check the dates, the registration window and the fee". Dev's earlier P0 build accepted this, and the reply lists no field, so the Tour Manager can't tell what to fix.

Reproduced directly on staging with three events (fee only: 200, rating only: 200, both: 200, neither: 422 competition.event_invalid, fields: {}). The ADD-25/47/48 checks were blocked by this; their setup now adds a rating so the checks themselves can run.

Medium · to investigate

Registration window in the venue's local time is refused, and the Overview shows no time zone

ADD-33: saving a registration window entered in venue wall-clock time returns 422 on staging (dev 200). The event Overview also shows no time-zone label on staging. Consistent across two runs.

Fixed during the run: on 22 Oct staging had no Stripe key ("Payments are not configured"), which blocked every payment test. By 24 Oct it was fixed: a Regional membership was paid with test card 4242 and both emails arrived. Report: artifacts/staging-payment-2026-10-22/

Where staging does better than dev

Pass on staging, fail on dev
  • Adult registration through membership, event entry, insurance and event payment (smoke).
  • Date of birth and dates follow the selected country (US, UK, Australia).
  • Payment retries: valid-card change, withdrawal and retry (live).
  • Payment: full recovery and scheduled reminder emails (live).
  • AUTO-AST-007: AL answers a calendar date query.

Most likely because staging still runs P0: these flows changed on dev with the P1 build, or dev has regressions there.

Same on both (known bugs, not a staging difference)

  • 13–17 and under-13 athletes see events before guardian consent (6 event cards). Same on dev and staging.
  • A child's email can't equal the guardian's (new rule on both). ADD-08 used the parent's email for the child; the test now leaves the child's email empty, as parents do in the app.
  • Eligibility isn't re-checked at Confirm & charge: an athlete who became ineligible is still charged. Open question Q2, seen on dev 19 Oct.

How it was compared

  • Every check's staging result was compared with its last result in the dev master report (tests/compare-env.mjs).
  • Failures caused by this computer's network (DNS lookups, dropped connections, timeouts) were rerun until they ran cleanly, or are listed under "Still to rerun".
  • Each remaining difference was checked by hand, often reproduced directly against the staging API, before it was called real.
  • Staging used its own accounts: two Tour Manager accounts and 20 SES-verified athlete inboxes. All payments in Stripe test mode.
