WSL-BUG-026 — Age 13–17 — event registration form opens before guardian consent

Status: Failed — tracked in Jira WSL-9587 (Teen event access is available before guardian consent).

Test case: com.worldsurfleague.registration.tests.SignupAgeThirteenToSeventeenE2ETest.registerAsThirteenToSeventeenAndCompleteGuardianFlow

Severity / Priority: High / P1 (proposed)

Classification: Observed application failure; root cause requires developer confirmation

What happened

The Taiwan Open of Surfing registration form opened, including coverage, attending guardian and entry confirmation sections. This check did not prove a completed entry.

What should happen

Before guardian consent, show a consent restriction and do not display the event entry form.

Why it matters

A minor can access an event entry form before the required guardian approval.

What needs to be done

Enforce the pending-consent restriction in the direct registration route and its server-side entry checks. Verify both under-13 and 13–17 sessions after the correction.

Steps to reproduce

  1. Register a 13–17-year-old athlete and send the guardian consent email.
  2. Keep consent pending: do not open or approve the guardian link.
  3. Continue through the membership steps used by the test.
  4. Open the known Taiwan Open of Surfing registration URL directly.
  5. Inspect whether the entry form is accessible.

Environment: WSL dev. Run started: 2026-10-14T21:12:25.025056900Z. Results and evidence are retained from that execution; this wording update did not rerun the test.
